# Sales-Analytics
## Recommended Structure and Order
### 1.	📊Project Title / Headline
Sales Analytics Performance Dashboard – End-to-End Business Intelligence Solution Using Power BI
An interactive analytical platform that transforms raw transactional sales data into actionable insights across regions, customers, products, and time periods.

### 2. 🎯Short Description / Purpose
The Sales Analytics Performance Dashboard is a data-driven Power BI solution created to provide a centralized view of company sales operations.
It enables business users to monitor revenue, profit margins, discount impact, customer contribution, and category performance through intuitive visualizations and dynamic filters.

The dashboard is designed for: <br>
Sales managers.<br>
Business analysts.<br>
Operations teams.<br>
Strategic decision-makers.<br>

to track performance, identify opportunities, and improve profitability.

### 3. 🛠Tech Stack
List the key technologies used to build the dashboard.

Example:
The dashboard was built using the following tools and technologies:<br>
•	📊 Power BI Desktop – Primary tool for building interactive reports, visuals, and dashboards.<br>
•	📂 Power Query – Data transformation and cleaning layer for reshaping and preparing the data.<br>
•	🧠 DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) – Used for calculated measures, dynamic visuals, and conditional logic.<br>
•	📝 Data Modeling – Relationships established among tables (Sales transactions,Customers,Products) to enable cross-filtering and aggregation.<br>
•	📁 File Format – .pbix for development and .png for dashboard previews.

### 4. 📂Data Source
Source: Retail Sales Transaction Dataset (CSV/Excel)

Sales data covering customer details, product categories, order information, regional performance, and financial metrics such as sales, profit, and discounts.
The dataset is structured to support time-based, customer-level, and category-wise analysis within Power BI.

### 5. ✨Features / Highlights
The best dashboard explanation format. <br>
•	Business problem<br>
•	Goal of the dashboard<br>
•	Walk through of key visuals (briefly!)<br>
•	Business impact & Insights

Example:<br>
•	Business Problem<br>
Organizations generate large volumes of sales data across regions, customers, and product categories, yet decision-makers often struggle to gain a clear, real-time view of performance.

Key questions such as:<br>
Which regions are driving the highest revenue and profit?<br>
Which customer segments contribute most to sales?<br>
Which product categories are most profitable?<br>
How are sales and profit trending over time?<br>
…are difficult to answer quickly using raw spreadsheets or static reports.<br>

• Goal of the dashboard<br>
To deliver an interactive sales analytics tool that:<br>

• Enables users to explore sales performance across regions, customers, and products.<br>
• Supports business decisions related to revenue growth, profitability, and market focus.<br>
• Uncovers trends in sales, profit, customer contribution, and category performance over time.<br>

• Walk through of key visuals (briefly!)<br>

~ Key KPIs (Top Section)<br>
Displays high-level business performance metrics:<br>
Total Sales Revenue<br>
Total Profit<br>
Profit Margin (%)<br>
Total Orders<br>
Average Discount (%)<br>
~ Region Filter Panel<br>
Interactive slicers allow users to filter all visuals by region, customer segment, and time period for focused analysis.<br>
~ Sales by Customer Segment (Pie Chart)<br>
Shows revenue contribution across Consumer, Corporate, and Home Office segments to identify major revenue drivers.<br>
~ Top Customers by Sales (Bar Chart)<br>
Ranks customers based on total sales to highlight high-value accounts and revenue concentration.<br>
~ Regional Sales Performance (Map Visual)<br>
Geographic visualization of sales and profit across regions, helping identify high-performing and underperforming markets.<br>
~ Yearly Sales & Profit Trends (Line Chart)<br>
Tracks business growth and profitability trends over time to support forecasting and performance monitoring.<br>
~ Category Performance (Grouped Bar Chart)<br>
Compares sales and profit across major product categories such as Technology, Furniture, and Office Supplies.<br>
~ Product-Level Detail Table<br>
Provides granular insights into individual product performance, including sales volume and profitability.<br>

• Business impact & Insights<br>

• Identifies high-performing regions and products.<br>
• Highlights profit drivers and discount impact.<br>
• Reveals top customers and key revenue segments.<br>
• Tracks sales and profit trends over time.<br>
• Supports smarter business decisions.<br>
